# Welcome to Followspot

You're reading the notes section. It's above the first rule, so the prompter never shows it. Put anything here you don't want on screen: links, reminders, the date you filmed. This file is the script Followspot shows when you haven't opened one of your own.

---

*[Welcome. Press Space, allow the microphone, and read this out loud.]*

Hi, and welcome to Followspot. This is a teleprompter that listens while you read. As you speak, the highlighted word follows your voice, and the script scrolls to keep your place on the reading line.

Try it now. Keep reading at your normal pace, and watch the highlight move along with you.

*[How it follows you]*

You don't have to be word perfect. If you skip a few words or say something a little differently, it catches up. If you stop talking, it waits for you. When you start again, it carries on from where you stopped.

If it ever loses your place, click any word to jump there. The left and right arrow keys move one word at a time, and the up and down arrows move a whole paragraph.

*[Writing your own script]*

A script is a plain text or Markdown file. A line of three dashes marks where the script starts, and a second one marks where it ends. Anything above the first or below the second is ignored, which makes it a good place for notes and a shot list. This file has notes at the top that you can't see here.

Stage directions go in square brackets. [Like this one.] They show up dimmed, and the prompter never listens for them, so you can leave yourself reminders without reading them out. Headings are skipped too, and each paragraph is a chunk you can jump to.

*[Before you record]*

Press S for settings, where you choose your microphone and camera. Press M to mirror the text for a beam splitter prompter, and F for full screen. Press R to go back to the top.

In the morning, shape it, drop it into a hot pot, and bake it. That's the whole recipe.
