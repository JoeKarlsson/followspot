# Demo script

Shown when no script is linked, and used to record the README demo.

---

*[Wide shot. Lights up on the kitchen.]*

Welcome back to the kitchen. Today we're making the easiest loaf of bread you'll ever bake, and it only needs four ingredients.

*[Cut to the counter.]*

Start with flour, water, salt, and a little yeast. Mix them in a big bowl until there's no dry flour left. It'll look shaggy, and that's fine.

Now cover the bowl and walk away. Give it twelve hours on the counter while you sleep.

In the morning, shape it, drop it into a hot pot, and bake it. That's the whole recipe.
